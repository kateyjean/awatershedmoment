I'm Katey, and my first love was the natural world.

My parents started taking me canoeing before I could walk. By the time I was in middle school, I had my own kayak and was paddling myself down the river beside them. Or in front of them (sorry, Mom). I participated in Stream Team around the same time, and planned my first parent-free float with classmates my senior year of high school. I moved to the seaside after college to work for the Artemis program, and now I do thermodynamics for steam generating heat pumps. My fiancé proposed to me on a kayak trip. You get the picture; my life revolves around water, so I do backyard hydrology. In my professional life I do a lot of staring at a screen, so I try to minimize that in my personal life unless it's really worth it to me. These days I like to garden, crochet, knit, sew, spend time with my dog, and do projects with my fiancé. My one great computer-related love is retro computing. I love anything that happened over a phone line, the history of digital communication, and the thrill of having to put it all together yourself. Unfortunately I don't have infinite space to dedicate to the Amigas and Apples I'd love to tinker with, but I do have the software experience to "do it myself" in that same spirit. I wrote an emulator for the Tandy CoCo 3, and I dream of one day setting up a bona fide modem-only BBS board, even if nobody ever dials in. I invite you to check out my projects on the home page to find out more about all of this.
